# Butler's Marina

Website for Butler's Marina (Butler's Waterfront & Marina), 409 Chester Avenue, Annapolis, MD 21403. A boutique marina on Back Creek in Eastport since 1998.

Built by Siani Creates. Static HTML/CSS/JS on Cloudflare Pages, with two Pages Functions.

## Files

- `index.html` – home
- `slips/`, `lifts/`, `overnight/`, `amenities/`, `leasing/`, `location/`, `faq/`, `contact/` – one folder per page
- `styles.css`, `script.js`
- `privacy/` and `tc/` – SMS privacy policy and messaging terms. These keep their old URLs (`/privacy/`, `/tc/`) because the text-messaging registration points to them. Wording is unchanged from the old site.

Every page shares one header, footer and phone bar. The pages are generated by a build script (`tools/site.py`, with the dock map in `tools/dockmap.py`), so a change to the header or footer is made once and rebuilt. Links are relative, so the site works on any host.
- `404.html`
- `_redirects` – old WordPress page URLs forward to the matching page
- `functions/api/inquiry.js` – the request form, writes to the Google Sheet through `tools/sheets-inquiry.gs`
- `functions/api/conditions.js` – live tide, water temperature and wind from NOAA station 8575512 (Annapolis), cached 6 minutes
- `functions/_middleware.js` – keeps `*.pages.dev` preview addresses out of Google and points share previews at whatever host serves the page
- `assets/img/` – photos, the burgee mark, share card
- `assets/fonts/` – Hanken Grotesk and Source Serif 4, self-hosted

## Deploy on Cloudflare Pages

1. Pages > Create > Connect to Git > `butlersmarina`, branch `main`
2. Framework preset: None. Build command: blank. Output directory: `/`
3. Settings > Variables and secrets (Production):
   - `SHEETS_URL` – the Apps Script web app URL for the requests sheet (ends in `/exec`)
   - `SHEETS_SECRET` – the same secret phrase written at the top of the script
4. Custom domain: `butlersmarinaannapolis.com` and `www`. Point DNS at Pages only when the site is approved, since this replaces the live WordPress site.

Until the Sheets variables are set, the form opens the visitor's email app with the request filled in, addressed to info@butlersmarinaannapolis.com.

## Requests sheet (Google Sheets)

Requests land in a Google Sheet the marina team already uses, on a tab named `Requests`. The columns are Received, Type, Name, Email, Phone, Boat, Length (ft), Beam (ft), Draft (ft), Arriving, Leaving, Message, SMS consent, Status, Notes and From page. Status starts as New and has a dropdown (New, Contacted, Quoted, Booked, Waitlist, Closed); the team updates Status and Notes by hand. The sheet is "Butler's Marina Requests", owned by butlersmarinamgmt@gmail.com. Deploy the script from that account so the notification emails come from the marina.

Setup, once:
1. Open the sheet, then Extensions > Apps Script. Paste in `tools/sheets-inquiry.gs`.
2. Change `SECRET` to a long random phrase and save.
3. Deploy > New deployment > Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Approve the permissions it asks for (edit this spreadsheet, send email as you).
4. Copy the web app URL into Cloudflare Pages as `SHEETS_URL`, and the secret as `SHEETS_SECRET`. Redeploy the site.

Each request also emails info@butlersmarinaannapolis.com, with reply-to set to the person who asked. The script creates the `Requests` tab with headers if it isn't there. If the script is edited later, use Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit > New version so the URL stays the same.

Text opt-ins are the rows with SMS consent = Yes. Zapier can watch the sheet for new rows with Yes and add them as contacts in Text-Em-All.

## Splash screen

The burgee unfurls once per visit (sessionStorage `bm-splash`), then lifts away after about two seconds. Tap or any key skips it. It never shows with reduced motion, and CSS ends it on its own if the script fails, so it can't trap a visitor. The logo is rebuilt as clean vectors: the burgee from its measured geometry with a redrawn anchor, and the type set in real PT Serif glyphs. `burgee.svg` (flag only), `logo.svg` (stacked, brand colors), `logo-light.svg` (stacked, for dark backgrounds), `logo-h.svg` (horizontal, for the header), `wordmark.svg` (type only, for the splash). The header carries the horizontal logo inline so its colors flip from light-on-photo to brand colors once the header turns white.

## Pages

- Home: photo hero with the availability bar, a sideways rail of cards (annual slips, boat lifts, overnight, leasing, winter), the "Boutique by design" split, a preview of the area map with walk times, and a closing call to action.
- Annual slips: fixed docks, boats 25 to 75 ft, quote on request, waiting list.
- Boat lifts: boat lifts for 7,000 to 30,000 lbs, jet ski lifts and small tender lifts.
- Overnight: $4 per foot per night with electricity included, upon availability, in three steps.
- Amenities, Leasing (long term residential and office space leasing), Location (chart, tide line, restaurants linked to Google Maps), FAQ, Contact.

The availability bar adapts to what the visitor picks. Overnight shows length, arrival and nights with a live estimate. Annual slip and boat lift show length only. Leasing hides the boat fields. Its button opens the contact page with the request filled in (`/contact/?type=…&loa=…&arrive=…&nights=…`). Any link to `contact/?type=Boat+lift` (or another type) preselects that request. On phones, a bar at the bottom keeps Call, Directions and Request a slip one tap away.

## Dock map

The Slips and Lifts pages carry an interactive map of the docks, traced from the 2011 condominium plat (Charles P. Johnson & Associates) and drawn as SVG by `dockmap.py`. Desktop gets a wide view with the main pier running left to right; phones get a tall view. Tap or press Enter on a slip to see where it sits and ask about it (`/contact/?type=…&slip=N` fills in the message). "Lift slips" dims every slip without a lift.

Lift slips are set in `LIFTS` at the top of `dockmap.py`: 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 20, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29. Change the set and rebuild when a lift is added or moved.

## Area map

The Location page has an interactive map of Eastport, downtown and the Naval Academy, drawn from OpenStreetMap data (`tools/areamap.py`). Filter by Eat and drink, Shops, Parks, Landmarks or Getting around; pick a place and the map zooms to the walking route from 409 Chester Avenue, with the walk time and a link to walking directions in Google Maps. Walk times are shortest paths over the OpenStreetMap street network at 3 mph, so they follow real streets and the Spa Creek bridge.

To add or move a place, edit `PLACES` in `tools/areamap.py` and rebuild. The street and shoreline data is a snapshot pulled November 2026; rebuild from a fresh pull if streets change.

## Openings

Apartment and office openings live in `OPENINGS` at the top of `script.js`. Add one line per opening; leave the list empty when nothing is open and the box asks visitors to get in touch.

## Tide line

Tide predictions for NOAA station 8575512 through December 31, 2027 are embedded in `location/index.html` (`#tideData`) and shown as one line under the map. Before the end of 2027, rebuild with a fresh year of predictions. `/api/conditions` is still in place if live readings are wanted later.

## The chart

The Location page has a map of Back Creek drawn from OpenStreetMap shoreline and pier data, projected into an SVG (1200 × 1057 units, lon −76.500 to −76.446, lat 38.945 to 38.982). Butler's sits at about (446, 379), checked against the US Census geocoder and OpenStreetMap. The orange course line is the `track` path; its waypoints are all in open water. The chart is marked "Not for navigation".

- The course line draws in from the Bay when the chart scrolls into view. It is fully drawn at rest, and reduced motion skips the animation.
- Phones get a tighter view of the creek (`viewBox` set in `script.js`).

## Changing rates or details

- Overnight rate: `RATE` in `script.js`, plus the "$4" on the home card, the Overnight page and the FAQ
- Slip size range: `MIN` and `MAX` in `script.js`, plus the copy on the Slips page and FAQ
- Lift sizes: Lifts page, Slips page, Amenities page and FAQ
- Check-in and check-out times: Overnight page and FAQ
