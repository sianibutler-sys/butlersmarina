Marcellous and Pier Butler tied up here in 1998.

They opened the marina on Back Creek that year, and the family has run it ever since. The next generation grew up on these docks.

Butler’s is smaller than most marinas on the creek, and that is the point. Fewer slips keep the docks quiet and the community close.

409 Chester Avenue, Eastport

Tie up for the season

Annual slips fit boats from 25 to 75 feet. The floating docks ride the tide with your boat, and lifts take the smaller craft out of the water between trips.

Winter on Back Creek

A de-icing system keeps the basin open through the cold months, so your boat can stay in the water all year. No haul-out and no spring launch date to wait on.

Questions we hear on the dock

When did Butler’s open?

In 1998. Marcellous and Pier Butler founded the marina, and the family still runs it.

Where exactly are you?

409 Chester Avenue, Annapolis, MD 21403. The marina sits on Back Creek on the Eastport peninsula, across Spa Creek from downtown Annapolis.

What size boats fit?

Boats from 25 to 75 feet. Send us your length, beam and draft and we’ll match you to a slip.

Can my boat stay in the water over the winter?

Yes. A de-icing system keeps the water open around the docks all winter.

How do overnight stays work?

Overnight dockage is $3 per foot per night with every amenity included. Slips open when annual tenants are away, so book ahead if you can, or join the waiting list if your dates are flexible. Check in is 1:00 PM and check out is by 11:00 AM.

Do you rent anything besides slips?

Yes. Apartments and office space on the property. Use the form below and pick the one you’re after.

Do you send texts?

Only if you opt in. Read our SMS privacy policy and messaging terms.
