Welcome to the dock.

We opened Butler’s on Back Creek in 1998 and kept it small on purpose. Fewer slips mean quieter docks, and neighbors who know each other’s boats by name.

Our family grew up on these docks. The fire pit still draws a crowd on summer nights, the de-icers still run all winter, and there is still a Butler to call when you need something.

    Our story starts in Galesville.

    1. 1930sGalesville, Maryland

      Margaret Crowner ran a two-room cook shop on the water, with no running water, feeding the oyster shuckers and crab pickers. She and her husband owned that waterfront when very few Black families could. The shop was where neighbors met to talk politics and watch movies.

    2. 1998Eastport, Annapolis

      Marcellous and Pier Butler open the marina on Back Creek. The same year, Eastport declared itself the Maritime Republic of Eastport, with the motto “We like it this way.”

    3. TodayStill family run

      The next generation grew up on these docks. The fire pit gathers neighbors the way Margaret’s shop did.

    A year on Back Creek

    Spring

    Commissioning season. Annapolis Yacht Club’s Wednesday night races start up in late April.

    Summer

    Long evenings on the grounds. The grills and the fire pit get their most use.

    Fall

    The U.S. Sailboat Show fills City Dock every October, across Spa Creek from Eastport.

    Winter

    The Eastport Yacht Club Lights Parade runs on the second Saturday in December. Back on the creek, the de-icers keep the water open.

    Questions we hear on the dock

    When did Butler’s open?

    In 1998. Marcellous and Pier Butler founded the marina, and the family still runs it.

    Where exactly are you?

    409 Chester Avenue, Annapolis, MD 21403, on Back Creek on the Eastport peninsula, across Spa Creek from downtown Annapolis.

    What size boats fit?

    Boats from 25 to 75 feet. Send us your length, beam and draft and we’ll match you to a slip.

    Can my boat stay in the water over the winter?

    Yes. De-icers keep the water open around the docks all winter.

    How do overnight stays work?

    $3 per foot per night, with every amenity included. Slips open when annual tenants are away, so book ahead if you can, or join the waiting list if your dates are flexible. Check in from 1:00 PM, check out by 11:00 AM.

    Is there a bathroom and laundry for slip tenants?

    Yes. Slip tenants have a full bathroom and a washer and dryer in the mixed-use building on the property.

    Do you rent anything besides slips?

    Yes. Apartments and office space in the same building. Use the form below and pick the one you’re after.

    Do you send texts?

    Only if you opt in. Read our SMS privacy policy and messaging terms.
